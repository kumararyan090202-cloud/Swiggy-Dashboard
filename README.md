# 🍔 Swiggy Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Swiggy Sales Dashboard** is an interactive data analytics project designed to analyze food delivery sales performance, customer behavior, restaurant performance, and order trends.

The dashboard transforms raw Swiggy-related data into meaningful business insights using interactive visualizations and KPIs. It helps stakeholders understand **what is selling, where sales are coming from, how customers behave, and which areas require attention**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Food delivery platforms generate large volumes of transactional data every day. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify:

* Which locations generate the highest sales?
* Which food categories and items are most popular?
* How are orders and revenue changing over time?
* What is the average order value?
* Which restaurants or outlets perform better?
* What are the major customer preferences?
* Which areas have opportunities for growth?
* Where are sales underperforming?

This dashboard addresses these challenges by providing a **centralized and interactive view of key business metrics**.

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard focuses on important business metrics such as:

| KPI                        | Description                              |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 💰 Total Sales             | Total revenue generated                  |
| 🛒 Total Orders            | Number of orders placed                  |
| 📦 Average Order Value     | Average revenue generated per order      |
| ⭐ Average Rating           | Average customer rating                  |
| 👥 Customer/Order Analysis | Understanding customer behavior          |
| 📍 Location Performance    | Sales and orders by location             |
| 🍕 Category Performance    | Performance of different food categories |

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales Analysis

Analyzes overall revenue and identifies sales trends over time.

### 2. Order Analysis

Tracks order volumes and helps identify periods with high or low demand.

### 3. Customer Analysis

Provides insights into customer preferences and ordering behavior.

### 4. Product & Category Analysis

Identifies popular food categories and products based on sales and order volume.

### 5. Location Analysis

Compares performance across different cities/locations to identify high-performing and underperforming regions.

### 6. Rating Analysis

Analyzes customer ratings to understand satisfaction patterns and their relationship with business performance.
